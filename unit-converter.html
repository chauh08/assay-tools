Unit Converter

Type a value in any field, then press Enter or Tab. Linked fields recalculate and turn blue. Molecular weight is needed to convert between molar and mass units.
Molarity ↔ mass concentration
Mass concentration = molarity × MW. For example, 10 mM of a 400 g/mol compound is 4 g/L (= 4 mg/mL).
Weigh-out: mass ↔ volume ↔ molarity
Uses the molecular weight above.
Editing mass or volume updates the molarity. Editing the molarity updates the mass needed for the current volume. Use this to work out how much powder to weigh for a stock.
Solvent needed for a target concentration
Solvent volume = mass ÷ (MW × concentration). Uses the molecular weight above. If you enter a known solubility limit, you get a warning when the target exceeds it. This assumes the compound fully dissolves and ignores volume change on dissolving.
