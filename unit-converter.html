Molarity ↔ mass concentration
Mass concentration = molarity × MW. For example, 10 mM of a 400 g/mol compound is 4 mg/mL.
Make a stock: how much solvent to add
Uses the molecular weight above. Solvent = mass ÷ (MW × concentration). Edit mass or concentration to get the solvent volume; edit the solvent volume to see the concentration you'd get. Assumes full dissolution and ignores volume change.
