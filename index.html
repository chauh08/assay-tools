Dispense Calculator

Type a value in any field, then press Enter or Tab. The other fields recalculate and are highlighted in blue. Enter a dose to get the dispense volume, or a dispense volume to get the dose.
Source & dispense
DMSO % = dose ÷ stock × 100; dispense = dose × volume ÷ stock, in 2.5 nL steps. After backfill: conc = dose × dose volume ÷ backfill volume. After transfer: conc × transferred volume ÷ total volume. Editing any result back-calculates the dose.
